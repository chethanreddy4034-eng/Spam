📐 How Naive Bayes Classifies Emails

Naive Bayes calculates the probability that an email belongs to Spam or Ham using the words found in the email.

1. Bayes' Theorem

P(Spam | W) = [P(Spam) × ∏ P(wᵢ | Spam)] / P(W)

2. Laplace Smoothing

P(wᵢ | c) = (Count(wᵢ,c)+α) / (Total Words+α×|V|)

3. Log-Space Computation

log P(c|W) = log P(c) + Σ log P(wᵢ|c)

4. The Naive Assumption

P(w₁,w₂,...,wₙ|c) = ∏ P(wᵢ|c)
