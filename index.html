📐 How Naive Bayes Classifies Emails

Multinomial Naive Bayes calculates the posterior probability that an incoming email belongs to category (Spam or Ham) given the sequence of observed words.

1. Bayes' Theorem

P(Spam | W) = [ P(Spam) × ∏ P(wᵢ | Spam) ] / P(W)

Calculates the posterior probability by multiplying prior class probability by the product of individual word likelihoods.

2. Laplace Smoothing

P(wᵢ | c) = (Count(wᵢ, c) + α) / (Total Words in c + α × |V|)

Prevents zero-probability multiplication errors when encountering words that never appeared in training data (setting α = 1.0).

3. Log-Space Computation

log P(c | W) = log P(c) + ∑ log P(wᵢ | c)

Multiplication of many small probabilities causes floating-point underflow. Computing in log-space replaces multiplication with addition.

4. The "Naive" Assumption

P(w₁, w₂, ..., wₙ | c) = ∏ P(wᵢ | c)

Assumes all word occurrences are conditionally independent given the email category. Despite its simplicity, it delivers remarkable classification accuracy!
